# DESIGN.md - YP HUB

## Identity
- Brand: YP HUB, a placeholder hub for projects and collaboration.
- Audience: potential clients, collaborators, and visitors.
- Tone: calm, professional, direct. No hype.

## Visual language
- Editorial and structured, not a template landing page.
- Warm off-white background, charcoal text, deep teal accent.
- Typography: system sans-serif for body and headings; weight and size create hierarchy.
- Spacing: generous vertical rhythm; sections separated by space, not cards.

## Dials
- ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)
- RHYTHM: 2 (Consistent with a few breaks)
- MOTION: 1 (Hover states only)

## Content rules
- Use labeled placeholders until real content exists.
- No fake statistics, testimonials, or logos.
- Navigation only to sections that exist on the page.
